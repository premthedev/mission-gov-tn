## 3. Aptitude baseline: 20 questions, 30 minutes

Pen and paper only. Pick one option for each question; don't leave any blank.

1. 48 ÷ 6 × 3 + 12 − 5 × 2 = ?  (A) 26 (B) 34 (C) 18 (D) 30
2. 25% of 480 + 12.5% of 640 = ?  (A) 200 (B) 180 (C) 220 (D) 240
3. A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. The net change is:  (A) no change (B) 4% increase (C) 4% decrease (D) 2% decrease
4. The price of rice rises by 25%. By what percent must a family cut consumption to keep spending the same?  (A) 25% (B) 20% (C) 15% (D) 12.5%
5. HCF of 84, 126 and 210:  (A) 14 (B) 21 (C) 42 (D) 84
6. LCM of 12, 18 and 30:  (A) 90 (B) 60 (C) 360 (D) 180
7. The least number that leaves remainder 5 when divided by 12, 15 and 20:  (A) 60 (B) 125 (C) 65 (D) 185
8. HCF of x² − 9 and x² + 5x + 6:  (A) x − 3 (B) x + 2 (C) x + 3 (D) (x + 3)(x − 3)
9. A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5. Then A : B : C =  (A) 2 : 3 : 5 (B) 8 : 12 : 15 (C) 6 : 9 : 10 (D) 4 : 6 : 5
10. ₹1,750 is divided in the ratio 3 : 4. The larger share is:  (A) ₹750 (B) ₹875 (C) ₹1,050 (D) ₹1,000
11. Simple interest on ₹8,000 at 6% per year for 3 years:  (A) ₹1,440 (B) ₹1,240 (C) ₹1,520 (D) ₹480
12. Compound interest on ₹10,000 at 10% per year for 2 years, compounded yearly:  (A) ₹2,000 (B) ₹2,100 (C) ₹2,210 (D) ₹1,100
13. Difference between CI and SI on ₹5,000 at 4% per year for 2 years:  (A) ₹4 (B) ₹16 (C) ₹10 (D) ₹8
14. A can finish a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. Working together they finish it in:  (A) 7.2 days (B) 7.5 days (C) 6 days (D) 15 days
15. A garden is 24 m × 18 m. A path 2 m wide runs all round it on the outside. Area of the path:  (A) 168 m² (B) 184 m² (C) 200 m² (D) 176 m²
16. Volume of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm (π = 22/7):  (A) 1,540 cm³ (B) 1,440 cm³ (C) 440 cm³ (D) 3,080 cm³
17. 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?  (A) 46 (B) 49 (C) 48 (D) 50
18. 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?  (A) 42 (B) 40 (C) 44 (D) 36
19. If CAT = 24 (add the letters' alphabet positions), then DOG =  (A) 24 (B) 25 (C) 26 (D) 27
20. Pointing to a man, Priya says: "He is the son of my grandfather's only son." The man is Priya's:  (A) father (B) uncle (C) brother (D) cousin

## 4. Thirukkural (45 min)

Open `THIRUKKURAL_20_CHAPTERS.md`.

1. **10 min:** learn the 20 chapter names in the order of the index table. TNPSC asks "இக்குறள் எந்த அதிகாரத்தில் உள்ளது?" (which chapter is this kural from?), so the names matter as much as the couplets.
2. **30 min:** chapters 1 and 2: **ஒழுக்கம் உடைமை (131 to 140)** and **பொறையுடைமை (151 to 160)**. For each kural: read it aloud twice, cover it, say the first line from memory, and say its meaning in your own Tamil.
3. **5 min:** self-test. Cover the page and write the first words of as many of the 20 kurals as you can.
